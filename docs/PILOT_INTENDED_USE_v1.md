## What Prism is (today)

A pilot-stage situational assessment. Candidates work through realistic
multi-party scenarios in conversation; an AI evaluation panel summarizes
observable evidence into five dimension profiles with per-dimension
consistency indicators.

## Intended use

- Decision-**support** for early-career screening conversations within the
  published job-family scope (JOB_FAMILY_SCOPE_v1) — entry-level
  individual-contributor roles in the two named families.
- A structured, evidence-quoting alternative to unstructured first-round
  impressions.
- Voluntary individual self-insight (₹499 B2C).

## Explicitly NOT intended (see PROHIBITED_USES_POLICY_v1)

- Not a sole or primary basis for hiring, admission, promotion or termination.
- Not a ranking instrument: no composite score is exposed; no cross-candidate
  leaderboards exist.
- Not validated for senior, specialist or safety-critical roles.
- Not a psychological, clinical or personality evaluation.
- No predictive-validity claim is made while studies remain PENDING.
