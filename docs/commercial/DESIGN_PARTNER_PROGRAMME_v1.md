# DESIGN-PARTNER PROGRAMME v1 — INTERNAL DRAFT

**DRAFT — commercial execution is human-gated: pricing HA-015, signed
partners HA-016, outcome-data agreements HA-017.** No partner names may be
claimed until agreements are signed and recorded in the register.

## Objective

3–5 paying design partners (institutions/employers) who run real cohorts,
give structured feedback, and — under separate data agreements (HA-017) —
contribute outcome data for criterion-validity studies (§17).

## What a design partner gets

- Pilot cohort(s) under the B2B package (invite links, seat/completion
  accounting, human-review allowance, cohort roster).
- Direct input into the interpretation guide and buyer surfaces.
- Pilot technical report per term (PILOT_TECHNICAL_REPORT_TEMPLATE_v1).
- Early-partner pricing (per approved pricing package — pending HA-015).

## What Prism gets

- Real assessment volume for calibration (per-item response accumulation).
- Structured feedback (template below).
- Optionally (HA-017): outcome data under a separate, counsel-reviewed
  agreement — never a condition of the pilot itself.

## Success-metric template (charter §22)

Per partner, per term — all measured, never estimated:

| Metric | Source | Target (provisional) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seats contracted vs used | invite accounting | ≥80% utilisation |
| Completion rate | §23 dashboard | ≥85% |
| Median assessment duration | telemetry | within design range |
| Human-review rate | disputes/allowance tracking | ≤ allowance |
| Candidate complaint rate | disputes + support | <2% |
| Buyer-reported usefulness | structured survey | ≥4/5 |
| Renewal intent | renewal fields on invites | stated in writing |

## Renewal / expansion tracking

Invites carry `renewalOf` (links a term's cohort to its predecessor) and
`plan.term` — renewals are queryable facts, not claims.
