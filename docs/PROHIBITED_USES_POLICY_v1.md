# PROHIBITED USES POLICY v1 (pilot)

**DRAFT — pending employment-law review (HA-004). Incorporated by reference
into pilot terms; the "not sole basis" canon is served verbatim from
`NOT_SOLE_BASIS_POLICY` in `server/lib/sharedConstants.js`.**

A buyer (institution or employer) participating in the Prism pilot may NOT:

1. Use a Prism result as the **sole or primary basis** for any admission,
   selection, employment, promotion or termination decision.
2. Construct **rankings or leaderboards** of candidates from Prism outputs
   (no composite exists on any buyer surface by design).
3. Set **cut-off scores** on any dimension.
4. Treat **"Insufficient evidence"** as a low score — it means not measured.
5. Infer anything from a candidate's **accommodation status**; alternate
   administration appears only as a neutral disclosure where materially
   relevant, and filtering by it is impossible by design.
6. Request or use **raw integrity telemetry**; only the tri-status
   (conditions met / review recommended / invalidated) exists, and it never
   feeds a score.
7. Use results **outside the published job-family scope** or for senior,
   specialist or safety-critical roles.
8. Require candidates to **pay** — institution-required candidates never pay.
9. Circumvent **candidate-authorized sharing** (share links are the only
   channel; scraping or credential-sharing breaches the terms).
10. Redistribute reports or use them for any purpose other than the
    candidate-facing decision process they were shared for.

Violations end pilot participation; candidates affected are notified where
legally required.
