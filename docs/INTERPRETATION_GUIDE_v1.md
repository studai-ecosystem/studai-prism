## Reading a Prism profile

- **Five dimensions, no total.** Prism reports a profile. There is no
  composite score, no percentile ranking against other candidates, and no
  pass/fail line. Comparing two candidates by "overall Prism score" is
  impossible by design and prohibited by policy.
- **Scores are evidence summaries.** Each dimension score (0–100) summarizes
  what an AI evaluation panel observed in one situational exercise, with
  quoted evidence. Read the quotes, not just the number.
- **AI panel consistency** tells you how much the independent AI reads
  agreed. Lower consistency = wider uncertainty; treat the score as a range,
  not a point.
- **"Insufficient evidence"** means the exercise did not produce enough
  observable evidence for that dimension (for AI & Digital Fluency this is
  strict: no direct probe answered → no score). It is NOT a zero and NOT a
  weakness signal.
- **Alternate administration** (when shown) means the assessment ran with
  approved accommodations that materially change interpretation context —
  it carries no information about why, and may not be used as a filter.
- **Integrity status** is contextual (conditions met / review recommended /
  invalidated) and never adjusts scores.

## What a difference means

Small numeric differences (roughly within the stated variation interval) are
noise, not signal. A 62 vs 58 on one dimension is not a meaningful gap.

## Appropriate next steps for a buyer

Use the profile to structure an interview: probe the quoted evidence, explore
the low-consistency areas yourself. The report is a conversation map — the
decision remains yours and must rest on more than this assessment
(NOT_SOLE_BASIS_POLICY).

## Candidate reading

Your report shows what the panel could observe in ~20 minutes. It is a
snapshot under pilot conditions, not a verdict on your abilities. You may
request one free human review if you believe it misrepresents you.
